# Privacy Policy for Kaboomble

_Last updated: June 27, 2025_

Kaboomble ("the app") is a mobile game. This privacy policy explains how Kaboomble handles your data and your privacy rights.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage

- **No Personal Data Collection:** Kaboomble does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or game data from your device. All gameplay and settings remain local to your device.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.
- **No Cloud Sync:** The app does not sync your data to any external server or cloud provider.

## 2. Data Sharing

- **No Data Sharing:** Kaboomble does not share any files, gameplay data, or personal information with any third party.

## 3. Analytics

- **No Analytics:** The app does not collect analytics or usage data. No information about your usage is sent to any analytics provider.

## 4. Advertising

- **Google Mobile Ads:** Kaboomble uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display ads. AdMob may collect device information (such as device ID, IP address, and ad interactions) to serve and personalize ads.
- **User Consent:** On first launch and as required by law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you are asked for consent regarding personalized ads. You can change your consent preferences at any time in the app settings.
- **No Personal Data Sharing:** Kaboomble does not share any gameplay or personal data with Google or any third party. Only ad-related device data is handled by Google, as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Permissions

- **Network:** The app uses network access only for loading ads.

## 6. Security

- **No Remote Storage:** Your data never leaves your device.

## 7. User Rights

- **Consent Management:** You can change your ad consent preferences at any time in the app settings.
- **Data Deletion:** Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Kaboomble does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data remains on the user’s device. If a child uses the app, all data is local and can be deleted by uninstalling the app. As the developer, I do not have access to any user data and cannot delete information on your behalf.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in this file.

## 10. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact the developer via the app store listing or the app's GitHub repository.

---

**Summary:**
- No personal data is collected or shared.
- No account, no cloud, no analytics.
- Ads are shown with your consent and do not access your gameplay data.
- You control your data at all times.
